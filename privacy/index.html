1. Who can use DRINKS

DRINKS sells alcohol, so you must be 18 or older to create an account. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up, and drivers check ID at the door before handing over any alcohol. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it.

2. What we collect

Customers

  • Name, email address, cellphone number and date of birth.
  • Delivery addresses and, if you allow it, your phone's location when you pin your address or choose the closest store.
  • Orders: what you bought, when, from which store, the amounts paid and any notes for the driver.
  • Proof that the driver checked your ID (the type of ID shown, not a copy or number).
  • Messages you send to our support team.

Drivers

  • Name, email address and cellphone number.
  • Vehicle type, make, colour and registration number, and driver's licence number and expiry date.
  • The driver's location while they are carrying an order, so the customer can see how far away they are.

Store staff

  • Name, email address and the store you work at.

Everyone

  • A notification token for your phone, so we can send order updates.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the service securely, such as sign-in records.

Card details: payments are handled by Paystack. We never see or store your full card number.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and confirm you are 18 or older.
  • To take, prepare, deliver and track your orders, and work out the delivery fee from your distance to the store.
  • To take payment and give refunds.
  • To let the store confirm which driver is collecting an order, and let you know which vehicle to expect.
  • To send order updates and answer support questions.
  • To meet our legal duties under liquor, tax and consumer protection laws, and to prevent fraud.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising by other companies.

4. Who we share it with

  • The store preparing your order: your name, phone number, delivery address, notes and items.
  • The driver delivering your order: your name, phone number, delivery address and location pin, and notes.
  • You, about your driver: their name, vehicle and registration number (never their licence number).
  • Service providers who run parts of DRINKS for us, under contracts that require them to protect your information:
    • Paystack (card payments and refunds)
    • Supabase (secure database and sign-in)
    • Expo, Apple and Google (sending notifications to your phone)
    • Our email and website hosting providers
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, for example liquor authorities or SARS.

5. Information stored outside South Africa

Our database is hosted in the European Union, and some of our service providers process information in other countries, such as the United States for notifications. We only use providers bound by data protection laws or contracts that give your information protection at least equal to POPIA, as section 72 of POPIA requires.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Orders and payments: 5 years, because tax law requires us to keep financial records. When you delete your account, we remove your name, phone number, addresses and location from these records and keep only what the law needs.
  • Driver location: only the latest position is kept, and it is only shared while an order is on its way.
  • Support messages: for as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 2 years.

7. Keeping it safe

We use encrypted connections, access controls so that customers, drivers, stores and staff only see what they need, and trusted providers. No system is perfect: if a breach ever affects your information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • ask what information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • correct your information (most of it you can change in the app);
  • delete your account at any time in the app: Account → Delete my account (or without the app);
  • object to how we use your information;
  • complain to us, and if you're not satisfied, to the Information Regulator.

Email help@drinksapp.co.za for any of these. We reply within 30 days.

9. Location

The app asks for your location only when you choose a store, pin your delivery address or, for drivers, while delivering. You can turn location off in your phone's settings; you can still type your address instead.

10. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app if the change matters to you.

Not for sale to persons under the age of 18. Drink responsibly.
