Delete your DRINKS account

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open DRINKS and sign in.
  2. Go to Account (drivers: Me; store staff: Store).
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. If you have an order on its way, finish or cancel it first.

Without the app

Email help@drinksapp.co.za from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my DRINKS account". We confirm it's you and delete the account within 7 days.

What we delete and what we keep

  • Deleted: your name, email, phone number, date of birth, saved addresses, location pins, notification tokens and login. Drivers' vehicle and licence details are deleted too.
  • Kept for 5 years, without your name or contact details: order and payment records, because South African tax law requires us to keep financial records.
